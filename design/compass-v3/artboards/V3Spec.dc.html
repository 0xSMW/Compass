Anchor rules

Same place, same job. Close top-left on every morning and evening screen, back top-left everywhere else. History top-left and Settings top-right on Today. Keyboard bottom-left. Skip bottom-right.
One dock line. Every bottom dock is centred on y 712: mic, answers, player, Note, Use this, Save, Continue. Today’s completion circle lives in the card, fixed at 604.
Text grows away from controls. Headers and focal text grow down from 128 or 224. The Today card grows up from 656. Transcripts scroll inside their region.
Turn-taking slots. Question → prompt → summary share 224. Mic → player share 712. Notes, live words and notices share the row above the card.
Planned reflow only. Keyboard lifts the action to 436. Typed text goes where spoken words would appear; if that spot is under the keyboard (Today’s notes), the field rides on the keyboard. At the largest sizes, circles become full-width buttons. The Today header sits in a scroll region from 104 down to 24 above the notes row, with 24 padding at top and bottom. At rest the goal starts at 128 and is never clipped. Only once scrolled does the nav get a solid backdrop.

Accessibility

Dynamic Type to AX5. Text reflows, controls keep their size and line, and choice circles become 72pt labelled buttons. Long headers scroll with 24 padding and start fully visible. Drawn on C11, C11b, B8, D7, D8, A8 and E6.
Every verb has two ways. Voice or keyboard for planning, notes and reflection. Tap for completion, never voice.
Contrast. All text ≥ 5.7:1 on ground. The missed mark (3.8:1) is a graphic, not text.
Screen readers. Every icon button is labelled. Recording announces “Listening” and “Saved”. Completion announces “Done, undo available”.
No hidden gestures. Every action on every screen is a visible control.
